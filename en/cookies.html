Cookie policy

Effective: November 4, 2026

This is a translation for convenience. If there is any difference, the Spanish version prevails.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser to remember information about your visit. Some are needed for the site to work; others are used to measure visits or show advertising.

2. What we use today

This site does not set any first-party or third-party cookies: we do not use analytics, advertising pixels, embedded social media or tracking tools. Fonts, images and videos are served from our own site, without connecting to outside services.

Like any website, our hosting provider (Vercel) receives technical data from each visit, such as the IP address and browser type, to deliver the page and protect it against abuse. We do not use that data to identify you or build profiles.

When you send the contact form, what you typed goes to our form provider (Web3Forms) so it reaches our email. This does not set cookies in your browser.

3. Third-party sites

If you follow a link to another site, for example our scheduling page on Microsoft Bookings, that site may use its own cookies under its own policy. We suggest you review it.

4. If this changes

If we ever use cookies that are not strictly necessary, such as analytics or advertising, we will update this policy and show you a notice so you can accept or reject them. Those cookies will not be switched on before you consent, and you can change your choice at any time.

5. How to control cookies

You can view and delete cookies in your browser settings. If you have questions, write to contacto@verticecollective.co. The processing of personal data is governed by our Privacy policy.
